Privacy Policy

The Papervoice app collects nothing.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no telemetry of any kind.

We do not know how many books you have, which voices you use, how long you listen, or that you have opened the app at all.

Your documents

The PDFs you import stay on your device. They are read on your phone, by software running on your phone, and are never uploaded anywhere. Neither is the text taken from them, nor the audio generated from that text.

This is not a promise about our intentions; it is a property of how the app is built. Papervoice is open source, and anyone may check: github.com/shashankappu21/papervoice

When the app uses the network

Once, per voice you choose to download.

Selecting a natural voice downloads its model from a public address named in the app's source code. That request contains no information about you or your documents beyond the fact that a file was requested. The app makes no other network requests, and works fully offline afterwards.

Permissions

  • Storage and file access — to open the PDF you pick. Nothing else is read.
  • Notifications — to show playback controls while a book is being read.

There is no microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.

The early-access list

This is the one place Papervoice holds anything about you, and it only happens if you ask it to.

If you give your email address on the home page to join the Android beta, that address is stored so an invite can be sent to you. Alongside it are the date you signed up and, when the link you arrived through carried them, the campaign tags in that link (which advert or post brought you here). Nothing else is asked for and nothing else is kept.

It is used to send you a beta invite, and messages about getting access — and for nothing else. There is no newsletter. It is never sold, and never shared with anyone beyond the service that stores the list and Google Play, which needs the address to issue the invite. Ask at any time and the address is deleted — or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

This website

The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties: the fonts and images are served from this domain. Your browser keeps any campaign tags from your link for the length of the visit so a later sign-up can be attributed, and that is cleared when you close the tab. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers see the usual request information, including your IP address.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, it will change here first, and the app's release notes will say so plainly. Anything less would make the rest of this document worthless.
