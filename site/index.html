What changes

Turn reading time into listening time.

Walk. Commute. Rest. Keep going. Papervoice lets the things you want to read come with you.

It picks up exactly where you stopped — to the sentence — in every book, however long you leave it.

Voices

Natural voices. On your device.

Choose from natural-sounding voices and download them once. After that, they work offline.

Lyra
US · female · on this phone

Lyra is the voice most people settle on. There are others — British and American, female and male — and your phone's own voice works too, with nothing to download.

Offline

No internet required.

Once a voice is downloaded, listening happens entirely on your phone. Aeroplane mode, the underground, a dead signal — it carries on reading.

Privacy

No uploads. No account.

Your documents stay on your device. Papervoice does not need your books in the cloud just to read them aloud.

That isn't a promise about intentions — it's how the app is built, and the source is public so anyone can check. Read the privacy policy

Questions

Before you sign up.

Is Papervoice free?

Yes. It is currently free and open source.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after downloading a voice once. Your phone's own voice needs no download at all.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. Reading happens on-device. The app fetches a voice when you choose to download one, and makes no other network requests.

Is it on Google Play?

Not publicly yet. Early-access users will be invited to the Android beta.

Can I install it from GitHub?

Yes, if you're comfortable sideloading an APK. Builds are published on the releases page, each with a checksum you can verify.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 and newer.
