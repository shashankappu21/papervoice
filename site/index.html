Install

Listening in three steps.

  1. 1Download the Android app.
  2. 2Open the downloaded file and follow Android’s installation prompts.
  3. 3Open Papervoice and add a PDF.

Natural voices are downloaded separately, from inside the app, once each. After that they work offline. Your phone’s own voice works straight away, with nothing to download.

More help installing

Apps from outside Google Play install only if you allow the app you downloaded with — usually your browser or file manager — to install them. Android asks the first time, and opens a setting called something like Install unknown apps. The wording and the steps vary between Android versions and phone makers.

That permission stays on for that app until you turn it off. Once Papervoice is installed you can switch it off again, in the same setting: Settings → Apps → your browser → Install unknown apps.

Android may also show a warning about installing an app from outside Google Play. Read what it says before continuing. If you would rather not install this way, request a Play Store beta invite instead.

Privacy

No uploads. No account.

Your documents stay on your device. Papervoice does not need your books in the cloud just to read them aloud.

Questions

Before you start.

Is Papervoice free?

Yes. It is currently free and open source.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after downloading a voice once. Your phone's own voice needs no download at all.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. Reading happens on-device. The app fetches a voice when you choose to download one, and makes no other network requests.

Is it on Google Play?

Not publicly yet, and the Google Play beta is still being prepared. You can download the Android app from this page now, or request a beta invite and get one when testing opens.

How do I install it?

Download the app, open the file, and follow Android’s prompts. Listening in three steps has the details, including the permission Android may ask for and how to check the download.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 and newer.
